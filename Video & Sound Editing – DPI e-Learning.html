Subject Outcome

Basic video editing, workspace, sequencing & real-time editing, motion with keyframes, effects & transitions, color theory & balance, title & caption with alpha, sound technology, sound taking, and rendering — with emphasis on practical work.

Reference: Digital Video Editing Fundamentals (Wallace Jackson) · Digital Audio Technology (Jan Maes) · Adobe Manual
